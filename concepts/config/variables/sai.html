SAI-specific variables

Variables read by the Broadcom SAI itself rather than the SDK. They live in the same config.bcm file. Most select a SAI policy or a resource budget that the SAI reports to SONiC; they do not change the size of a hardware table.

Set in several SONiC Trident 3 HWSKU files: sai_brcm_sonic_acl_enhancements, sai_brcm_sonic_trap_group, sai_eapp_config_file, sai_fast_convergence_support, sai_fdb_entry_l2_discard_src_enable, sai_interface_type_auto_detect, sai_load_hw_config, sai_mdio_access_clause22, sai_nbr_bcast_ifp_optimized, sai_optimized_mmu, sai_pfc_defaults_disable, sai_postinit_cmd_file, sai_preinit_cmd_file, sai_stats_support_mask, sai_tunnel_support, sai_tunnel_underlay_route_mode, sai_verify_incoming_chksum.
